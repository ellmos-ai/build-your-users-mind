## Binding rule

- Real, open decisions for `<USER>` are tracked in this chain, one entry per decision.
- Host-specific decisions may live in a separate `TO-DECIDE-<HOST>.txt`; a host also
  reads the global chain for decisions that are not host-specific.
- Only a filled-in `DECISION:` line counts as authorization. A captured entry without
  one stays open, however old.
- After verified implementation, move the full entry to `DECIDED-AND-DONE.template.md`.
- Project-specific decisions may additionally and preferentially be tracked in that
  project's own `DECISIONS.md` / `TO-DECIDE.md` / `DECIDED-AND-DONE.md`. Escalation to
  this chain is only needed for decisions with cross-project or operator-wide scope.
- **Cut-and-clue:** once this file exceeds roughly 150 active lines, split sequentially
  into `TO-DECIDE_2.txt`, `_3.txt`, and so on. Each part carries absolute
  predecessor/successor pointers so the chain stays walkable end to end.
